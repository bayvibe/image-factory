## Design Context

### Users
Xiaohongshu creators and casual social posters who want to make a sharp two-panel "yes/but" image quickly, usually from two existing photos or screenshots.

### Brand Personality
Fast, editorial, playful. The interface should feel like a compact creative tool rather than a landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, studio-like workspace with confident contrast, warm surfaces, and a few saturated controls. The generated image is the focus; surrounding UI should stay quiet and precise.

### Design Principles
- Keep the first screen usable immediately: preview, upload, text controls, and download are all visible.
- Make cropping tactile with direct manipulation on the image, plus simple zoom controls.
- Protect text readability on any uploaded image with shadow, stroke, and local contrast overlays.
- Optimize output for Xiaohongshu sharing with a fixed 3:4 export canvas.
